An Instapaper alternative with free Send to Kindle

Instapaper is in maintenance mode and its Send to Kindle is paywalled. Later gives you Send to Kindle and EPUB for free, in an actively developed open-source app.

Move from Instapaper to Later

  1. In Instapaper, open Settings and choose Export to download a CSV of your saved articles.
  2. Start Later and open the import screen.
  3. Choose Instapaper as the source and upload the CSV.
  4. Folders and saved URLs are imported and extracted into the Later reader.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Instapaper export?

In Instapaper, go to Settings and use Export CSV.

Is Send to Kindle free in Later?

Yes, Send to Kindle and EPUB export are free.

Can I keep using Instapaper while I try Later?

Yes. Exporting does not remove anything from Instapaper.

Do I need to self-host?

Self-hosting is available today; a hosted version is coming soon.
