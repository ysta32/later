Looking for an Omnivore alternative? Try Later

Omnivore shut down in November 2024. Later is open source like Omnivore was, with highlights, notes, tags, full-text search, and a one-command self-host.

Move from Omnivore to Later

  1. Use the export zip you downloaded from Omnivore. It contains your saved items and metadata.
  2. Start Later and open the import screen.
  3. Choose Omnivore as the source and upload the zip.
  4. Your articles, highlights and tags are brought over. Review your library and re-save anything that failed to extract.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move from Omnivore to Later?

Upload your Omnivore export zip in the Later import screen.

Will my highlights come across?

Later imports Omnivore exports including your saved items; review your library after import to confirm highlights and tags.

Can I run Later myself?

Yes. One Docker command starts it on port 4800 with a persistent volume.
