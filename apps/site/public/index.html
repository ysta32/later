Save it for later. Actually read it later.

Later is an open-source read-it-later app built around capture that never fails and a reader that is a pleasure to use. Pocket and Omnivore are gone; your reading list does not have to be.

Why now

Pocket shut down

Pocket closed in July 2025. Move your saves to Later.

Omnivore shut down

Omnivore closed in November 2024. Import your export.

Instapaper is in maintenance mode

And Send to Kindle is behind a paywall. See the difference.

Readwise Reader has no free tier

Users also report failed captures on news sites. Compare.

Everything you need to save and read

Capture that never fails

Server-side extraction, plus a browser extension that captures the rendered page from your own logged-in session, so paywalled and JavaScript-heavy pages still save.

Save from anywhere

Share sheet and PWA, email-in, RSS and newsletters, and a bookmarklet.

A reader you will want to use

Clean typography, light and dark themes, highlights and notes, tags, and full-text search.

Offline PWA

Install it on your phone or desktop and read your saved articles without a connection.

Free Send to Kindle and EPUB

Send articles to your Kindle or export them as EPUB. No paid tier required.

Text-to-speech

Listen to articles instead of reading them.

Optional AI, never required

Summaries and ask-your-library powered by Claude when you add a key. Without one, everything else works the same.

Import and export

Bring your library from Pocket, Omnivore, Instapaper, Readwise, or browser bookmarks. Export to Markdown or JSON, with Obsidian sync.

Self-host in one command

One Docker container and one volume. Your data stays yours.

Self-host in one command

Run Later on your own machine or server. Your library lives in a single Docker volume.

docker run -p 4800:4800 -v later-data:/data ghcr.io/ysta32/later

Then open http://localhost:4800. Read the self-hosting guide.

Open source, so it can not be shut down on you

The code is public and your data is exportable to Markdown and JSON at any time.
