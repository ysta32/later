A free Readwise Reader alternative

Readwise Reader has no free tier, and some users report failed captures on news sites. Later is free to start, open source, and captures from your own logged-in browser session.

Move from Readwise Reader to Later

  1. In Readwise Reader, export your library as CSV.
  2. Start Later and open the import screen.
  3. Choose Readwise as the source and upload the CSV.
  4. Your saved documents are imported. Re-save any page that failed using the browser extension.

Frequently asked questions

How do I export from Readwise Reader?

Export your library as CSV from Reader, then upload it in the Later import screen.

Why do captures fail on some sites?

Server-only extraction can be blocked by paywalls or script-rendered pages. Later also offers a browser extension that captures the rendered page from your own session.

Is Later a full replacement?

Later covers saving, reading, highlights, tags, search, and exports. Compare the table above against what you use.

Does Later cost money?

Later is open source and free to start. A hosted version is coming soon.
