The best Pocket alternative: Later

Pocket shut down in July 2025. Later is an open-source read-it-later app with the same save-and-read flow, plus highlights, full-text search, free Send to Kindle, and self-hosting.

Move from Pocket to Later

  1. Export your data from Pocket at getpocket.com/export. This produced a CSV of your saved URLs, titles, tags and timestamps. Pocket has shut down, so use an export you already downloaded.
  2. Start Later (see the self-hosting guide) and open the import screen.
  3. Choose Pocket as the source and upload your CSV file.
  4. Your saved links are fetched and extracted by the server so they are readable in the Later reader. Tags are preserved.

Frequently asked questions

Can I still export from Pocket?

Pocket shut down in July 2025. If you downloaded your CSV from getpocket.com/export before then, you can import it into Later. If you did not, check whether you have any other export or browser bookmarks, which Later can also import.

What does Later import from Pocket?

Saved URLs, titles, tags and timestamps from the Pocket CSV export.

Is Later free?

Later is open source and free to start. Self-hosting is available today; a hosted version is coming soon.

Does Later need AI?

No. AI summaries and ask-your-library are optional, and everything else works without them.
